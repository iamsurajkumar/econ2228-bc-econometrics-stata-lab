ECON2228 Econometrics: Stata Lab

Boston College, Fall 2026 · Suraj Kumar's lab sections · every lab answers one policy question with real data from a published study.

How to use this page
  1. Slides: read them before or after the lab.
  2. Do-files: download, change the cd line at the top to your own folder, and run in Stata from top to bottom.
  3. Notebooks (blue buttons): open in your browser, nothing to install. The first load takes about 30 seconds. Open in molab runs the same notebook on a free server where you can also edit the code (sign in with Google or GitHub).
